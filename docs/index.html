Cohort Retention by Month — % of Original Cohort Returning
📈 Average M1 retention of 20.6% sits at the low end of the 20–30% industry benchmark. The Dec 2009 cohort shows anomalously high retention (35%+) — this is an existing-customer data artifact, not a true new cohort. The M12 anniversary reorder spike confirms habitual seasonal wholesale buyers.
Key Findings & Business Recommendations
🔴
Churn Crisis
41.2% of customers have churned, representing £2.1M in lost revenue. Win-back email campaigns targeting the 535 At-Risk customers (avg £1,771 each) should be priority #1.
💡
80/20 Rule in Action
Loyal customers (30.9% of base) drive 80% of all revenue at £7,115 avg. A loyalty programme protecting this segment is the highest-value investment available.
📅
Seasonal Revenue Peaks
Q4 (Sep–Nov) consistently drives 40%+ of annual revenue. Supply chain, inventory, and marketing spend should be front-loaded to Q3 to capture peak demand.
👋
First-Purchase Conversion
27.6% of customers never return. A post-purchase email sequence (Days 7, 14, 30) targeting second-order conversion could materially lift LTV and payback on CAC.
